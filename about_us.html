About Our Royal Kitchen

Welcome to Rajwada Rasoi, where we bring the grand traditions and majestic flavours of Rajasthan straight to your plate. Inspired by the magnificent palaces, timeless culture, and rich heritage of the land of kings, our restaurant is designed to make you feel like royalty.

Our journey began with a simple vision: to preserve and celebrate the authentic culinary secrets of Rajasthani households and royal kitchens. Every dish we serve is prepared with love, using traditional cooking methods and hand-picked spices that capture the true essence of Marwar and Mewar.

Our signature dishes like melt-in-your-mouth Dal Baati Churma and rich Gatte ki Sabzi guarantee a dining experience that satisfies both the heart and the soul. At Rajwada Rasoi, it is not just about food; it is about celebrating the spirit of warm hospitality—"Padharo Mhare Desh"—that Rajasthan is famous for.
